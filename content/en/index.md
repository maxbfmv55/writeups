# Welcome to my site! 👋

<br>

This is my personal project where I’ll be sharing my **write-ups** of CTFs and practice machines.  
Here I document the steps, reasoning and lessons learned so anyone can follow the process and track my evolution as a pentester.

<br>
Right now I'm focused on machines that help prepare two entry-level certification tracks:  
<br>
<br>

> 🎯 **eJPTv2 — eLearnSecurity Junior Penetration Tester (INE/eLearnSecurity)**  
  A hands-on exam aimed at validating the fundamentals of pentesting (networking, web, enumeration and escalation).  

> ⚔️ **PT1 — Junior Penetration Tester (TryHackMe)**  
  A practical certification by TryHackMe designed to measure pentesting skills in real-world styled scenarios with content oriented towards employability.  

<br>

In each section you’ll find a listing of machines, a link to my write-up here and a link to the original challenge (TryHackMe, HackTheBox, etc.).  
This helps me organize my study by certification and maintain a clear track record for recruiters and peers.
